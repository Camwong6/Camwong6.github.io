San Francisco Chamber of Commerce (SFCC) Marketing Newsletters

Marketing Performance Analysis & Content Development

Overview

I wrote and optimized email newsletters for the San Francisco Chamber of Commerce, covering member outreach, event promotion, and community engagement. I used heatmap data to guide layout decisions and coordinated with multiple internal teams to align messaging.

Rebranding & Content Development

I joined SFCC during a major rebranding effort and worked closely with the marketing team to ensure all newsletters reflected the new brand identity, maintaining consistency in tone, layout, and messaging across communications.

  • I aligned newsletters with the updated brand guidelines.
  • I worked with the marketing team to maintain consistency in tone, layout, and messaging across all materials.

Newsletter Content Analysis & Optimization

Working directly with newsletter content, I used engagement insights to guide improvements in layout and content prioritization.

  • I used heatmaps to analyze engagement patterns across each newsletter.
  • I identified high-performing sections and drop-off points to guide layout decisions.
  • I repositioned content to increase visibility of key sections.

Event Promotion & Strategy

Heatmaps showed where readers' attention concentrated in each newsletter. I moved high-priority events into those zones to maximize visibility.

  • I cross-referenced heatmap hotspots with event placement to identify optimal positioning.
  • I prioritized events based on engagement data, not editorial instinct.
  • I tracked repositioned events to measure click-through lift.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

I coordinated with multiple teams to promote small businesses and initiatives including Yes SF and the San Francisco Center for Economic Development.

  • I kept messaging aligned across teams with different outreach goals.
  • I drafted copy for Yes SF and SFCED campaigns within the newsletter format.
